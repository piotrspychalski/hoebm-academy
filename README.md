# Sports Academy: interactive schematic figures on endurance-sport physiology

**Author:** Piotr Spychalski, MD, PhD — surgeon, clinical researcher and cyclist · House of EBM
**Live:** https://piotrspychalski.github.io/sports-academy/

Sports Academy explains the physiology of endurance sport with interactive, schematic figures, in the evidence-based-medicine style of House of EBM. Each figure is a "journal figure" (FIG. n · series, panels A/B/C, axes with units, a caption with sources) that you can step through frame by frame, move with sliders, and record as a 9:16 reel.

**The rule:** every number shown on screen is tied to a source in the figure registry, with a verbatim quotation and its verification date. Shapes of curves are illustrative and labelled as such. A figure with any unverified number is not published.

## Series

Gut · Heat & cold · Altitude · Training · Nutrition · Masters · Own data

## Features

Series and search, favourites, frame-by-frame steps with keyboard and presentation-clicker control, a description panel (aim, key points, what we do not know, references by role), sources and "How to cite", mobile layout, and a clean recording mode (`#record`) for Instagram Reels with the Reels safe zone (`S`).

## Important disclaimer

Sports Academy is a **schematic educational tool**, not medical, nutritional or training advice, and not a medical device.
